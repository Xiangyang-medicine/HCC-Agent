**Supplementary Figure S3 | Cross-platform input compatibility and
outcome-blind standardisation for exploratory external transport.** **a,**
Screening flow for the available GEO cohort-platform strata. GSE14520 GPL3921
and GSE116174 GPL570 entered analysis; GSE14520 GPL571 was excluded because
only 21 cases had complete overall-survival data and no performance analysis
was conducted. **b,** Number of eligible uniquely target-mapped probes per
prespecified gene before median collapse. All 15 genes were available on both
included platforms. **c,** Principal-component analysis (PCA) of the
median-collapsed 15-gene matrices before cohort-wise standardisation. **d,**
PCA after outcome-blind gene-wise z-scoring within each cohort-platform
stratum. PCA panels are input quality-control visualisations and do not imply
biological equivalence. **e,** Distribution of frozen M2T gene-component risk
scores in the two eligible cohorts. Score distributions are not directly
calibrated across platforms. Probe mapping and standardisation did not use
survival outcomes. The external analysis assesses exploratory transport of the
frozen gene-only component and does not constitute external validation of the
full combined M4 model.
