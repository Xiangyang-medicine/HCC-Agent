# Supplementary Figure S3 contract

## Scientific claim

The prespecified 15-gene component can be constructed on two eligible
microarray cohort-platform strata after outcome-blind probe mapping and
within-cohort standardisation. This is an input-compatibility and transport
quality-control figure, not evidence that the full M4 model was externally
validated.

## Panels

- **a**: cohort screening and locked reporting scope.
- **b**: eligible uniquely mapped probe coverage for all 15 genes.
- **c**: raw collapsed-expression PCA.
- **d**: PCA after outcome-blind within-cohort gene-wise z-scoring.
- **e**: frozen M2T score distributions in included cohorts.

## Reporting boundaries

- GSE14520 GPL571 is shown only as `N=21; insufficient sample size; no
  performance analysis`.
- No GPL571 performance estimate is used or shown.
- External outcomes were not used for probe mapping or standardisation.
- PCA is input QC and does not establish biological equivalence.
- The transported component is the frozen gene-only M2T component, not M4.
