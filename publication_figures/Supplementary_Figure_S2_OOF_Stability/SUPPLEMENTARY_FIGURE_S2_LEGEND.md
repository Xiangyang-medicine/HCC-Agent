**Supplementary Figure S2 | Repeated out-of-fold prediction stability and model behavior.**  
**a,** Pairwise Spearman correlations between patient risk percentiles from the five repeated outer cross-validation runs. Each point is one of the ten repeat pairs; boxes show the interquartile range and median. **b,** Patient-level standard deviation of the five OOF risk percentiles. Violin shapes show all 363 patients per model; thick lines indicate the interquartile range and points indicate medians. **c,d,** Joint density of patient risk percentiles across all ten repeat pairs for M4 (c) and M5 (d), comprising 3,630 paired observations per model. The dashed identity line represents perfect repeat-to-repeat rank agreement; darker hexagons indicate greater density. **e,** Distribution of modal-quintile agreement across five repeats. For each patient, the modal quintile was identified and the number of repeats agreeing with that mode was counted. The inset reports structural checks applied to all 9,075 OOF prediction rows: survival probabilities were bounded in [0,1] and non-increasing across 12, 36 and 60 months. All panels are descriptive; no inferential testing was performed. Risk scores were converted to within-model, within-repeat percentiles before stability analysis. M1, clinical Cox model; M2, gene-only elastic-net Cox model; M3, combined clinical–gene elastic-net Cox model; M4, combined random survival forest; M5, DeepSurv.
