**Supplementary Figure S1 | Time-dependent discrimination and repeated out-of-fold calibration.** **a,** Time-dependent AUC at 12, 36 and 60 months for all five models. Light points denote individual outer test folds (25 per model and horizon); connected points and error bars show mean ± SD. **b–d,** Calibration of the clinical M1 reference and selected M4 model at 12 (b), 36 (c) and 60 (d) months. Within each of the five repeated OOF runs, 363 patients were divided into six equal-frequency bins of predicted event probability. Observed event probability was estimated as one minus Kaplan–Meier survival at the fixed horizon. Light lines show repeat-specific curves; dark lines and points show across-repeat means by risk bin, with error bars denoting SD across repeats. The dashed identity line denotes perfect calibration. Repeated predictions were never pooled as independent patients. Calibration displays are descriptive. M1, clinical Cox model; M2, gene-only elastic-net Cox model; M3, combined clinical–gene elastic-net Cox model; M4, combined random survival forest; M5, DeepSurv.
