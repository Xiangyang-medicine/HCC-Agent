# Figure 3 legend

**Fig. 3 | Exploratory cross-platform transport of the frozen
metabolic-gene prognostic component.** **a,** Kaplan–Meier estimates in the two
external cohorts. Higher- and lower-risk groups were defined using the median
M2T score in the locked TCGA-LIHC derivation cohort (−0.010); this threshold
was fixed before external outcomes were accessed and was applied unchanged to
both microarray cohorts. In GSE14520 GPL3921, the higher-risk group comprised
113 cases and the lower-risk group 108 cases (HR=2.13, 95% CI 1.37–3.31;
log-rank P<0.001). In GSE116174 GPL570, the corresponding groups contained
35 and 29 cases (HR=2.47, 1.08–5.66; log-rank P=0.027). Shading denotes
95% confidence intervals; vertical ticks denote censored observations. Numbers
below the plots show patients at risk. No external cutpoint search was
performed. GSE14520 GPL571 (N=21) was not analysed because of insufficient
precision. **b,**
Cohort-specific association between the transported continuous risk score and
OS. Hazard ratios (HRs) are reported per 1-SD higher risk score using a
univariable Cox model. GSE14520 GPL3921 yielded HR=1.58 (95% CI,
1.26–1.99; P<0.001), and GSE116174 GPL570 yielded HR=1.47 (0.99–2.20;
P=0.057). External outcomes were used only for this evaluation; no risk
threshold was selected and the frozen model was not recalibrated. **c,** Frozen
standardized log-hazard coefficients for the prespecified 15-gene input panel.
Elastic-net penalization retained non-zero coefficients for LDHA and PKM; the
remaining candidate coefficients were zero. No coefficient was refitted using
external outcomes. **d,** Cohort-specific Harrell and Uno concordance
statistics. Points show estimates and error bars show 95% patient-bootstrap
confidence intervals from 1,000 valid resamples. GSE14520 GPL3921 yielded
Harrell C=0.629 (95% CI, 0.571–0.687) and Uno C=0.635 (0.574–0.696);
GSE116174 GPL570 yielded Harrell C=0.603 (0.492–0.711) and Uno C=0.601
(0.483–0.712). The dashed line denotes chance discrimination (0.5). Cohorts
were evaluated separately and were not pooled. These analyses assess a
secondary exploratory cross-platform gene-only transport model. They do not
constitute external validation of the RNA-seq-plus-clinical M4 model, clinical
utility, deployment readiness or patient-level treatment benefit.
