# Table 1 title and notes

**Table 1. Characteristics of the development and external transport cohorts.**

Values are *n* (%) unless otherwise indicated. IQR, interquartile range; OS,
overall survival. Observed time is the recorded OS/censoring duration and is
not a reverse Kaplan–Meier estimate of follow-up. The two microarray cohorts
were used only for secondary exploratory gene-only cross-platform transport;
they are not formal external validation of the TCGA combined random survival
forest model. External outcomes were not used for model fitting, coefficient
estimation, feature selection or cutpoint selection. GSE14520-GPL571
(*N*=21 complete-OS cases) was excluded before analysis because the sample size
was insufficient. NA indicates that the characteristic was not available in
the locked analysis input; no value was imputed.
